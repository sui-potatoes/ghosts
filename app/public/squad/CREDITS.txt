COMMANDER / LOCAL SQUAD PREVIEW

Native body, facial targets, skin, eyes, brows, lashes and hair:
MakeHuman Community system assets, CC0 1.0.
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
Offline authoring: MPFB 2.0.18. The GPL Blender extension is not shipped.
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/v2.0.18

Frozen idle pose: existing native-rig retarget of Quaternius Universal Animation
Library Idle_Loop, CC0 1.0.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

Spectre light armor, weapon attachment calibration, cosmetics and the pistol:
reused from the existing Commander Black Rain prototype. Rifle geometry is the
Rifle mesh extracted from that prototype's blackline-operative.glb; sniper uses
that geometry with the prototype's optic/barrel concept. No new character or
animation library was downloaded for this integration.

The shared inventory and personnel records in this preview are fictional local
frontend data. The armor and weapon models remain prototype art.

Combat boots, utility belts and revised fitted shoulder caps: original
procedural geometry authored for this game, fitted to the native MakeHuman body.
Reproducible Blender source: game/scripts/build-field-kit.py.

NYX Veil / Shade / Wraith armor: original fitted geometry and procedural woven /
ceramic normal maps authored for Ghosts. Separate male/female native-bind exports.
Reproducible Blender source: game/scripts/build-nyx.py, native_gear.py and nyx_details.py.

Office worker / extraction VIP outfit: original procedural clothing authored for
Ghosts on the native MakeHuman body and skeleton. Separate male/female exports;
no additional third-party clothing or textures. Reproducible Blender source:
app/scripts/build-office-worker.py (uses native_gear.py). The relaxed civilian
idle adapts the existing CC0 weight-shift clip with lowered, empty hands.
