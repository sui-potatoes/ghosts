# Voice test batch: operative emotions and reactions

Ghosts voice: grim cyberpunk noir. Mercenaries working a corporate contract in a
rain-soaked city. Short lines, radio-clipped, nobody wastes words. Each block has
a delivery note for the voice model, then six lines that climb in intensity.
`voice-test-reactions.json` carries the same lines for a batch run.

## 01 · Calm acknowledgment

Delivery: flat, professional, low volume, no rise at the end.

1. Copy.
2. Moving.
3. On it. Keep the lane clear.
4. Understood. Holding this corner.
5. Reading you. Two steps, then I set.
6. Orders received. Nothing here but rain.

## 02 · Alert, contact spotted

Delivery: sharp intake, faster, clipped consonants, still controlled.

1. Contact.
2. Movement, left. Second floor.
3. Eyes on one. Armed. He hasn't seen us.
4. Two hostiles, forty meters, behind the van.
5. Hold. Hold. Something's wrong with that doorway.
6. Three of them. They're already spreading out. We're late.

## 03 · Fear, pinned down

Delivery: breath audible, words tumbling, pitch up, cracks on the loud ones.

1. They've got the angle.
2. I can't move from here.
3. Rounds in the wall, right by my head.
4. Somebody put fire on that window, please.
5. I'm pinned, I'm pinned, I'm pinned!
6. Get me out of this. Get me out of this now!

## 04 · Pain, wounded

Delivery: grunt first, then speech through clenched teeth, uneven pacing.

1. Agh.
2. Took one. Still up.
3. Leg. It's the leg. I'll walk it off.
4. That's through the plate. That's bad.
5. Okay. Okay. Don't look at it. Keep moving.
6. Can't feel my hand. Someone take my spot.

## 05 · Anger

Delivery: low and hard, then full voice. Consonants hit.

1. Enough.
2. You picked the wrong street.
3. Come on, then. Come on!
4. They shot her. They shot her and laughed.
5. I am done being careful with these people.
6. Burn it. Burn the whole relay. I don't care what Helios pays.

## 06 · Grief, ally down

Delivery: very quiet, slow, long pauses. No crying, the opposite of crying.

1. Bishop's down.
2. He's not moving. He's not moving.
3. Stay with me. Stay with me. No. No.
4. Copy. Leaving her. We don't leave people.
5. I had the shot. I had the shot and I waited.
6. Mark the body. We'll come back for him. We won't. Mark it anyway.

## 07 · Relief

Delivery: one long exhale, then warmer tone, half a laugh allowed.

1. Clear.
2. That's the last one.
3. We're good. We're actually good.
4. Hah. I thought that was it for me.
5. Okay. Okay. Everybody breathe.
6. Ghost, you beautiful lunatic, you hit that from where?

## 08 · Triumph, cocky

Delivery: grin audible, slight swagger, dry not loud.

1. Down.
2. Clean. Next.
3. Told you I'd make it.
4. That's one for the invoice.
5. Thirty meters, moving target, rain. Put it in the report.
6. Tell Helios they owe me a drink. Tell them twice.

## 09 · Dry humor, sarcasm

Delivery: deadpan, slower, a little tired. The joke is in the pause.

1. Lovely weather.
2. Great plan. Loved the plan.
3. Oh good. More of them.
4. Nobody said anything about a second team.
5. Sure. I'll just walk through the open street. What could happen.
6. Contract says "light resistance." Someone should tell the resistance.

## 10 · Doubt, hesitation

Delivery: unsure, trailing off, questions that aren't really questions.

1. Are we sure about this?
2. That's a long way across open ground.
3. I don't like it. I don't know why. I don't like it.
4. If we go now, we go without Echo.
5. Say again? You want me where?
6. I'll do it. I'm just saying, on record, that this is a bad idea.

## 11 · Determination

Delivery: steady, chest voice, each sentence lands and stops.

1. We finish this.
2. One more push. That's all it is.
3. I'm not leaving without the core.
4. Whatever's in that building, it's ours now.
5. Stay on me. Short bounds. Nobody gets fancy.
6. They have the numbers. We have each other. That's going to be enough.

## 12 · Exhaustion

Delivery: hoarse, slower, sentences lose energy at the end.

1. Still here.
2. Yeah. Yeah, I'm moving.
3. Three nights. Three nights of this.
4. I'll sleep when the invoice clears.
5. Just tell me where to stand.
6. Keep talking. If you keep talking I keep walking.

## 13 · Urgency, shouting commands

Delivery: full voice, projected, fast, every line an order.

3. Flank right, now, go!

5. Fall back to the truck! Everyone, back to the truck!

## 15 · Dying, last words

2. Tell them it was quick.

4. Take my tags. Take my tags.
5. It's fine. It's fine. Finish the job.

## 16 · Attack, charge

Delivery: rising aggression, forward momentum, breath driving the words. Starts
as a decision, ends as a roar.

1. Going in.
2. Push. Push now, while they're reloading.
3. On me. Straight through the gate. Don't stop.
4. They're breaking, they're breaking, hit them!
5. Charge! All of you, charge!
6. Come get it! Come get it, Kōra! I'm right here!

## 17 · Firing, calling the shot

Delivery: controlled aggression, breath held then released on the shot. Short,
percussive, no shouting until the target drops.

1. Taking the shot.
2. Lined up. Firing.
3. Target. Breathe. Squeeze.
4. Hit. He's still up. Again.
5. Burst, burst, burst. Down. Next one.
6. That's a kill. That's a kill. Hostile at the van is done.

## 18 · Grenade toss

Delivery: a warning shouted to friends, then the throw with effort in the voice,
then a beat of waiting before the blast.

1. Frag out.
2. Grenade! Heads down!
3. Cooking it. Three. Two. Away.
4. Over the van. Watch the splash, Bishop, you're close.
5. Eat this! Eat this!
6. Thrown. Thrown. Wait for it. Wait for it. There.

## 19 · Reload

Delivery: matter-of-fact under pressure, hands busy, words in the gaps.

1. Reloading.
2. Dry. Swapping mag.
3. Cover me, I'm empty.
4. Last magazine. Last one. Make it count.
5. Come on, come on, seat, seat. Good. Up.
6. Reloaded. Back in it. Sorry, where were we.

## 20 · Overwatch, armed and triggered

Delivery: armed lines are patient and low. Triggered lines snap, surprised
into action.

1. Overwatch set.
2. Watching the street. Nothing moves without me knowing.
3. Anyone steps into that lane, they're mine.
4. Movement. Taking it.
5. There! Got him on the crossing!
6. Reaction shot, reaction shot, he's down, he never saw the lane!

## 21 · Hunker down

Delivery: braced, compressed, speaking from behind something solid.

2. Going low. Wake me when it's over.
3. Head down, back to the wall, let them waste it.
4. I'll be a brick for a turn. Don't need me, don't call me.
