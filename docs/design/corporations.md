# Corporations (idea, not built)

A longer-term direction for the world around the matches. Nothing here is built
or decided yet.

## Factions

Three corporations offer missions. A squad works for any of them, and finishing
a mission pays out either a fixed reward or a random one (a loot box).

## Influence in the world

Each corporation has a public influence score, which moves as missions for it
are completed. A corporation that is losing ground can offer bigger rewards to
win it back, so the world pushes players toward whoever is behind.

## Standing with a corporation

Each player also has a personal score with each corporation. That standing sets
their reputation there and affects the rewards it offers them.

## Open questions

- What a mission is: PvE, PvP matches fought under a contract, or both.
- Whether influence rises only from a corporation's own missions, or also falls
  when its rivals' missions succeed.
- How the random rewards stay fair, and how they sit alongside match credits.
- Whether standing with one corporation costs standing with the others.
